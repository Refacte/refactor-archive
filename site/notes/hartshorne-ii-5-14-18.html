Normality, Coherent Extension, Tensor Operations, and Affineness

Choose algebras, modules, and local sections that make the constructions in Hartshorne II.5.14–18 explicit, then verify the gluing back to geometry.

These notes bring together exercises 14 through 18 in Chapter II, Section 5 of Hartshorne’s Algebraic Geometry. The emphasis is on the relationships between the constructions: normalization and section rings, coherent extension, tensor operations, relative Spec, and vector bundles.
