The mechanism

Make the remainder carry the obstruction

A proposed countable partition into closed sets is tested by looking at what remains after its pieces are removed. Closedness in the real line is essential. The argument uses a nonempty closed remainder and Baire category to expose the contradiction.

What becomes elementary: The global partition question is concentrated into a topological obstruction on a carefully chosen remainder.

Keep ambient closedness distinct from relative closedness, and check nonemptiness before applying Baire category.
