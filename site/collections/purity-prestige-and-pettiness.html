Purity, Prestige, and Pettiness

An annotated collection on pure mathematicians’ status games, contempt for useful work, and the habits that let prestige substitute for judgment.

The polemics, recollections and disputes collected here examine how mathematical authority is used to belittle people and their work. Original arguments appear alongside responses, with passage locators and notes on what each source establishes.

Read the claims in context

The focus is the use of pure-mathematical prestige to turn a preference into a verdict on someone else’s worth. The collection includes direct criticisms, the original statements they answer, and broader work on professional recognition. The opening essay follows the argument across these materials.

Each entry identifies its form and access status. Some links provide complete texts, while others supply excerpts, translations or bibliographic leads. The reading notes distinguish a participant’s recollection, a polemical interpretation and an institutional study.
